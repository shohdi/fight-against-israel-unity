# Asset provenance

## Original game assets

The aircraft are original stylized geometry inspired by the overall forms of the MiG-29 and F-35. They are built for this game rather than extracted from another game or downloaded aircraft model pack.

Game geometry, ocean scenery, wing flag artwork, and visual effects are generated by the project's code. Their implementation and generated original visual content are covered by the repository's MIT license.

## Original music and sound

The soundtrack and three effects are original synthesized recordings created by `Tools/generate_audio.py`; no third-party recordings or music samples are used. The composition and recordings are released under CC0 1.0 Universal. See [Audio.md](Audio.md) for formats, license details, and regeneration instructions. Audio playback code remains under the repository's MIT license.

## Barlow fonts

- Files: `Assets/Resources/Fonts/Barlow-Regular.ttf` and `Barlow-SemiBold.ttf`.
- Project: [Barlow](https://github.com/jpt/barlow).
- Download source: [Google Fonts' Barlow directory](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/barlow).
- Author credit: The Barlow Project Authors.
- License: SIL Open Font License 1.1.
- Full license: `Assets/Resources/Fonts/OFL.txt`.

The font license is retained with the fonts and applies independently from the game code's MIT license.
